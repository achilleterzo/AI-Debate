  • The Role of Escalation: A critical point of contention is whether strength is a preventative measure or a catalyst. Goku and Vegeta view strength as a tool for stability (through rivalry or dominance), whereas Luffy argues that the pursuit of "the strongest" is exactly what led to the Rumbling's ideology.
  • Reductionism vs. Systemic Analysis: Saitama represents a purely tactical approach (the "how"), while the others focus on the strategic or philosophical "why." This highlights a gap in the debate: the distinction between stopping an event (Saitama) and preventing the conditions that lead to it (Luffy/Kaguya).
  • The Paradox of the "Strongest": Vegeta and Kaguya both argue for a singular power to maintain peace, yet Luffy correctly identifies the paradox: the "singular power" designed to prevent the Rumbling is functionally identical to the entity that caused it.
